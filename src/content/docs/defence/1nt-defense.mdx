<BiddingTree>
(1!n)
- Dbl: !s + another suit (or GF bal if after 1!c (1!n))
    - P: Convert to penalty
    - 2!c: P/C
    - 2!d: NF with !h tolerance
    - 2!h/!s: To play
- 2m: !h + m
    - P/2!h: Preference
    - Raise: Blocking
    - Suit: To play
    - 2!n: Strong ask
        - 3m: Min. 4=!h
        - 3om: Max. 4=!h GF
        - 3!h: Min. 5=!h
        - 3!s: Max. 5=!h GF
- 2M: 5+M Nat
- 2!n: 5+!c 5+!d
- 3m: If after 1!c (1!n), 5+!h 5+m GF, else nat preemptive
- 3M: Nat. Long suit. 8 tricks
</BiddingTree>

<Aside type="note">
Applies for direct seat, pass-out seat, passed hand, or any modes when opponents interfere with a natural 1!n and we have not shown a real suit.

Bid agressively (4-4 okay) opposite strong NT NV. Bid constructively (rule of 20) opposite weak NT.

Dbl denies a good 5-card !s suit. 2m denies a good 5-card !h suit

Third seat redouble is ignored.
</Aside>
