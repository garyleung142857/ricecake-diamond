## General Rule when opponent bid

Facing opponents' bid when we haven't bid / bid 1!c / bid 1!c - 1!d
- Michael Cuebid (2X) is on if
    - opponent has made a 1-level bid (or doubling 1!c to show !c), and
    - bidder's partner can be 0 point
- If Michael Cuebid is off, that cuebid means GF hand without a good suit to bid
- 2NT is natural only after opponent opens (2M) or (1M) P (2M)
- Unusual 2NT is on if Michael Cuebid is on and 2NT is not natural
- Transfer Lebensohl is on if 
    - 2NT is not natural, and
    - Michael Cuebid is off, and
    - intervention is below (exclude) 2N
- Jump suit is preemptive if the bidder and his partner did not promise strength
    - If the bidder promised strength, jump bids shows max and good suit
    - If the bidder's partner promised strength, jump bids shows GF value and good suit (same if [Transfer Lebensohl](/modules/lebensohl-related/#transfer-lebensohl-tleb) is on)
