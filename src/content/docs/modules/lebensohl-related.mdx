## Basic Rules
- 2!n is always F1R as a response to a double of 2M
- We don't use any kind of Lebensohl / Rubensohl when we have bid a suit

## Standard Lebensohl (SLEB)
- Partner has made a takeout / strength showing double / double of multi 2m
- RHO has length
- The double must be followed by an opponent's pass, making the responder a compulsory position to act.
- The responder did not act before.
- Eg:
    - (2M) P (P) Dbl; (P) ?
    - (2!d=multi) P (2!h=P/C) Dbl; (P) ?
    - (1M) P (2M) Dbl; (P) ?

## Reversed Lebensohl (RLEB)

- Partner has made a takeout double / strength showing double / double of multi 2m
- LHO has length
- The responder did not act before
- Eg:
    - (2M) Dbl - ?
    - (2!d=multi) Dbl - ?
    - (1M) Dbl (2M) ?
    - (1M) Dbl (2M-1=good raise) ?

<BiddingTree>
Reversed Lebensohl Continuation
- 2X: Natural. NF
- 2!n: Inv+ F1.
    - 3m: NF. A minimum double. May have 4oM
        - 3-Cue: Ask stopper
        - 3oM: Inv
    - 3-Cue: GF. 4=oM (or 5=oM if you chose Dbl over bidding it; or 3=oM if no other good alternatives)
    - 3oM/4m: GF/ST. Was too strong to overcall
    - 3!n: To play
- NJS: Nat. Not invite
- 3-Cue: GF Ask stopper
- JS: Inv 5+ suit
- 3!n: To play
</BiddingTree>


## Transfer Lebensohl (TLeb)

- At least one of us showed strength and none of us has not shown a suit (eg. 1!c / 1!d / 1!n / 1!c - 1!d)
- Opponent bids a suit at 2 level.
- The responder did not act before.
- Eg:
    - 1!n (2M) ?
    - 1!c - 1!n (2M) ?
    - 1!n (2!d=long M) ?
    - 1!c (2M) ?
    - 1!d (2M) ?

<BiddingTree>
Transfer Lebensohl continuation
- Dbl: T/O
- 2X: To play
- 2!n: Any s/o, or !c invite, or to play in 3!n with stop
    - 3!c: Normal Response. Reject !c invite
        - P/3X: To play
        - 3-Cue: Ask 4-card major. With stop
        - 3!n: To play. Stopped
    - 3!d: Accept !c invite. P/C
- 3-Cue: Without ask stoper. Ask 4oM
- 3!c/!d/!h/!s: Inv+ in next suit (GF if showing !c)
    - 3-Shown-suit: NF
    - Others: GF. Could be Stopper probe
- 3!n: Want to play. No stop
</BiddingTree>

## Four Hearts 2!n

Two specific sequences (Can be preceeded by we opened 1!c / 1!d / 1!c - 1!d)
- (1!s) Dbl (2!s) Dbl; (P) ?
- (1!s) Dbl (P) 2!s; (P) ?

<BiddingTree>
Four Hearts 2!n continuation
- 2!n:  4=!h. Can have longer minor. F1R
- 3m: Nat. No 4!h
- 3!h: 5+!h GF
- 3!s: GF. Bal or both minors
- 3!n: To play. Was too strong for 1!n overcall
- 4m: 6+m one suited.
- 4!h: 6+!h to play
</BiddingTree>

## Scramble 2!n

- Game is impossible from the auction
- There are at least 2 possible suits to play in at 3-level
