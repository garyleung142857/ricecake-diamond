## Basic Rules
- 2!n is always F1R as a response to a double of 2M
- We don't use any kind of Lebensohl when we have bid a suit

## Transfer Lebensohl (TLeb)
- Partner has made a takeout / strength showing double / double of multi 2m
- RHO has length
- The responder did not act before.
- Eg (responder is forced):
    - (2M) P (P) Dbl; (P) ?
    - (2!d=multi) P (2!h=P/C) Dbl; (P) ?
    - (1M) P (2M) Dbl; (P) ?
- Eg (responder is not forced)
    - 1!n (2M) ?
    - 1!c - 1!n (2M) ?
    - 1!n (2!d=long M) ?
    - 1!c (2M) ?
    - 1!d (2M) ?

    - Others: Nat GF
- 3-Cue: Without stopper. Ask 4oM
- 3!n: Want to play. No stop
- 4!c/!d: Long !h/!s

## Reversed Lebensohl (RLEB)

- Partner has made a takeout double / strength showing double / double of multi 2m
- LHO has length
- The responder did not act before
- Eg:
    - (2M) Dbl - ?
    - (2!d=multi) Dbl - ?
    - (1M) Dbl (2M) ?
    - (1M) Dbl (2M-1=good raise) ?

<BiddingTree>

- 2X: Natural. NF
- 2!n: Inv+ F1.
    - 3m: NF. A minimum double. May have 4oM
        - 3-Cue: Ask stopper
        - 3oM: Inv
    - 3-Cue: GF. 4=oM (or 5=oM if you chose Dbl over bidding it; or 3=oM if no other good alternatives)
    - 3oM/4m: GF/ST. Was too strong to overcall
    - 3!n: To play
- NJS: Nat. Not invite
- 3-Cue: GF Ask stopper
- JS: Inv 5+ suit
- 3!n: To play
</BiddingTree>


## Four Hearts 2!n

Two specific sequences (Can be preceeded by we opened 1!c / 1!d / 1!c - 1!d)
- (1!s) Dbl (2!s) Dbl; (P) ?
- (1!s) Dbl (P) 2!s; (P) ?

<BiddingTree>
Four Hearts 2!n continuation
- 2!n:  4=!h. Can have longer minor. F1R
- 3m: Nat. No 4!h
- 3!h: 5+!h GF
- 3!s: GF. Bal or both minors
- 3!n: To play. Was too strong for 1!n overcall
- 4m: 6+m one suited.
- 4!h: 6+!h to play
</BiddingTree>

## Scramble 2!n

- Game is impossible from the auction
- 2!n bidder is forced to act
- There are at least 2 possible suits to play in at 3-level

## Natural 2!n

Specific sequences:
- (2X=Preemptive) 2!n
- (2X=Preemptive) P (P) 2!n
- (1M) P (2M) 2!n

2!n Natural system on.
