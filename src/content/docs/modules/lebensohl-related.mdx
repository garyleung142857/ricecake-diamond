## Basic Rules
- 2!n is always F1R as a response to a double of 2M
- We don't use any kind of Lebensohl when we have bid a suit
- We don't use any kind of Lebensohl when game is impossible from the auction (2!n is scramble)

## Reversed Lebensohl

- Partner has made a takeout double / strength showing double / double of multi 2m
- Responder is forced to act (pass will convert to penalty)
- The responder did not act before
- Eg:
    - (2M) Dbl - ?
    - 1!n (2M) Dbl (P); ?
    - (2M) P (P) Dbl; (P) ?
    - (2!d=multi) P (2!h=P/C) Dbl; (P) ?
    - (1M) P (2M) Dbl; (P) ?

## Rubensohl
- At least one of us showed strength and none of us has not shown a suit (eg. 1!c / 1!d / 1!n / 1!c - 1!d / T/O double)
- Opponent bids a suit at 2 level.
- Responder did not act before.
- Responder can pass
- Eg:
    - 1!n (2M) ?
    - 1!c - 1!n (2M) ?
    - 1!n (2!d=long M) ?
    - 1!c (2M) ?
    - 1!d (2M) ?
    - (1M) Dbl (2M) ?

<BiddingTree>
Rubensohl continuation
- Dbl: T/O. Competitive. Continue like [reversed lebensohl](#reversed-lebensohl)
- 2X: To play
- 2!n: 6+m comp of 5+!c inv+
    - 3!c: Normal response
        - 3!d: To play
        - 3-unbid-M: Natural GF
    - 3!d: !c support
    - Higher: (Only after Dbl) Strong case. Natural GF
- 3!c/!d/!h/!s: Inv+ in next unbid suit (GF if showing !c)
- Xfer cue: Ask stop if opponent shown one known suit, else shows stop in the transfer suit
- 3!n: To play
- 4!c/!d: Long !h/!s
</BiddingTree>


## Four Hearts 2!n

Two specific sequences (Can be preceeded by we opened 1!c / 1!d / 1!c - 1!d)
- (1!s) Dbl (2!s) Dbl; (P) ?
- (1!s) Dbl (P) 2!s; (P) ?

<BiddingTree>
Four Hearts 2!n continuation
- 2!n:  4=!h. Can have longer minor. F1R
- 3m: Nat. No 4!h
- 3!h: 5+!h GF
- 3!s: GF. Bal or both minors
- 3!n: To play. Was too strong for 1!n overcall
- 4m: 6+m one suited.
- 4!h: 6+!h to play
</BiddingTree>

## Scramble 2!n

- Game is impossible from the auction
- 2!n bidder is forced to act
- There are at least 2 possible suits to play in at 3-level
